1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CIPHERTRACE X is a criminal network intelligence and investigation platform engineered 
specifically for Indian Law Enforcement Agencies (State Police Cyber Cells, Central Intelligence 
Agencies, CFSL Forensic Labs, and Public Prosecutors). 

The platform transforms raw multi-modal evidentiary artifacts (First Information Reports, Telecom 
Call Detail Records, Banking & UPI Transaction Ledgers, Interrogation Transcripts, Seizure Panchnamas, 
and Digital Forensic Extractions) into an interactive, mathematically sound, court-admissible criminal 
knowledge graph with automated GraphRAG reasoning, multi-perspective hypothesis evaluation, and 
statutory legal chargesheet generation under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) 2023 and Section 63 
of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) 2023.


2. CORE PHILOSOPHY & WORKSTATION DESIGN DIRECTIVES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- "Don't make it look like an AI website. Make it look like the software that an investigator would actually use."
- ZERO decorative marketing fluff: No purple neon gradients, pill buttons, fake counters, fake reviews, or 3D cards.
- Restrained High-Contrast Workstation Theme: Deep slate/charcoal tones (#070a10, #080c14, #0d131f, #1e293b).
- Rectangular Ergonomic Controls: High-density layouts, clear loading/empty/error states, tabular grids.
- 4-Tier Semantic Relationship Taxonomy:
    1. OBSERVED  [Emerald Solid Line]: Direct physical/forensic evidence (CDR call, bank transfer, seized hardware).
    2. INFERRED  [Sky Blue Dashed Line]: Deterministic analytical link (shared address, co-location, co-travel).
    3. PREDICTED [Amber Dotted Line]: Machine Learning probabilistic inference with confidence percentage.
    4. CONTESTED [Rose Alert Hashed Line]: Disputed link flagged for judicial / defense scrutiny.
- 5-Level AI Epistemic Distinction:
    [EVIDENCE]    -> Grounded factual artifact with byte-offset citations and SHA-256 hash.
    [INFERENCE]   -> Deductive logical chain derived from multiple corroborating facts.
    [PREDICTION]  -> Probabilistic graph ML link classification with explicit confidence threshold.
    [UNCERTAINTY] -> Missing data disclosures, telecom coverage gaps, and potential spoofing warnings.
    [HUMAN VERIFIED] -> Sworn Investigating Officer (IO) attestation and override audit stamp.

4. 19-STAGE END-TO-END INVESTIGATION WORKFLOW
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Stage 01: Create Case Registry
  - Initializes official police case record with FIR number, jurisdiction, priority (Critical/High/Medium/Low),
    crime classification, and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) officer assignments.

Stage 02: Multi-Modal Evidence Fabric Ingest
  - Ingests raw data streams: FIR JSON/text, Telecom CDR CSVs, Bank Ledgers, Forensic Device extractions.
  - Automatically computes immutable SHA-256 cryptographic hashes and records acquisition provenance.

Stage 03: Extract Entities (LEA NER + Pattern Extraction)
  - Extracts Persons, Aliases, Phone Numbers, Devices (IMEI/IMSI), Bank Accounts, UPI IDs, Crypto Wallets,
    Vehicles, Locations, and Legal Sections with character byte-offset grounding.

Stage 04: Resolve Entities & Alias Disambiguation
  - Applies Jaro-Winkler, Soundex, and Double-Metaphone phonetic string matching to cluster aliases.
  - Implements Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) review: uncertain matches require recorded IO approval before merging.

Stage 05: Build Criminal Knowledge Graph
  - Projects all entities, calls, money transfers, co-locations, and transactions into a Neo4j property graph.

Stage 06: Visualize 4-Tier Network Topology
  - Renders the interactive criminal network graph with explicit visual semantics: Observed (Solid), 
    Inferred (Dashed), Predicted (Dotted), and Contested (Hashed).

Stage 07: Detect Communities & Syndicate Clusters
  - Executes the Louvain / Leiden modularity optimization algorithm to isolate sub-cells, mule rings,
    and command hierarchies within the syndicate.

Stage 08: Identify Bridge Nodes & Cut Vertices
  - Calculates Betweenness Centrality to pinpoint critical Hawala middlemen, corrupt facilitators, and
    communication gateways connecting disparate criminal factions.

Stage 09: Predict Hidden Links (Graph ML)
  - Evaluates topological proximity (Resource Allocation, Jaccard Index, Preferential Attachment) to uncover
    clandestine relationships not directly visible in captured logs.

Stage 10: Detect Temporal & Mule Account Anomalies
  - Analyzes high-frequency CDR burst swarms, sudden velocity spikes, rapid fund fragmentation (layering),
    and immediate ATM cash extraction drain patterns.

Stage 11: GraphRAG Evidence Retrieval & Semantic Citations
  - Combines vector embeddings with multi-hop graph traversals to provide grounded answers to investigator
    queries with exact evidence pointers.

Stage 12: Multi-Perspective Reasoning & Consensus Synthesis
  - Scrutinizes investigative hypotheses across 6 independent viewpoints: Lead IO, Forensic Specialist,
    Legal Prosecutor, Defense Alternative Theory, Suspect Exculpatory View, and Common-Sense Coherence.

Stage 13: Counterfactual Evidence Ablation Testing
  - Systematically removes specific evidence items to simulate "What if this phone log is ruled inadmissible?"
    and tests if the remaining evidence still establishes the criminal nexus beyond reasonable doubt.

Stage 14: Investigative Priority Scoring
  - Synthesizes flight risk, asset dissipation velocity, weapon/cyber threat index, and evidentiary maturity
    into an actionable 0-100 numerical lead priority score.

Stage 15: Next Best Action (NBA) Directives
  - Generates concrete, chronological operational directives for field officers (e.g. "Freeze Bank Account X 
    under Section 106 BNSS", "Subpoena Tower CDR for Cell Node Y").

Stage 16: Indian Legal Intelligence (BNS 2023 & BSA 2023)
  - Maps factual evidence directly against statutory ingredients of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (e.g. BNS 318(4) 
    for cheating, BNS 111 for organized crime syndicate activity) with proof checklist validation.

Stage 17: Human Challenge & IO Override Controls
  - Allows investigating officers to challenge or override any AI inference, node categorization, or link, 
    preserving defense dispute logs in the audit ledger.

Stage 18: Cryptographic SHA-256 Merkle Chain & Section 63 BSA Certificate
  - Seals all evidence, extraction logs, and graph mutations into an immutable Merkle tree.
  - Generates a court-admissible electronic record certificate satisfying Section 63 BSA / 65B IEA.

Stage 19: Police Investigation Dossier & Final Chargesheet Report
  - Compiles a complete, formatted judicial document featuring executive summaries, key player dossiers,
    Louvain community breakdown, financial flowcharts, legal charges, and blockchain verification seals.


5. STATUTORY LEGAL FRAMEWORK (INDIAN CRIMINAL JURISPRUDENCE)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023: Replaces IPC 1860; automated concordance for criminal conspiracy, 
  cheating, cyber fraud, and organized syndicate operations.
- Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023: Replaces CrPC 1973; governs search, seizure, property 
  attachment, and arrest procedures.
- Section 63, Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023 (formerly Section 65B IEA 1872):
  Mandates automated cryptographic checksum generation, device serial logging, forensic extraction tool
  documentation, and officer attestation for all digital evidence presented before the Court of Session.
- Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023: Enforces strict LEA statutory purpose limitation and
  tamper-evident access auditing.
